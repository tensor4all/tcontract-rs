## 2026-10-10 the TCCG class where the packed driver is behind BLIS, through TBLIS

Giving the campaign's `tcbench` cell an independent reference (tcontract-benchmark #10,
over tcontract-rs #94) made a class of rows visible for the first time. Over 196 rows
(16 MiB, 1T and 4T, 49 cases, two dtypes) the planner is ahead of TBLIS on 180, and
behind on 16 - on 14 of those by more than the row's own scatter - by up to 1.52x.
They are the cases whose contracted axes are interleaved with output axes inside an
operand: `abjc-cbka-kj`, `ajbc-ckba-jk`, `adbjc-cbdka-kj`, `ajbdc-ckbad-jk` and
`ijk-il-jlk`.

| Question | Ruling | Evidence or next check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Is the planner choosing the wrong route, or is the f64 `FaerLimit` mis-set? | **No, and no bound can fix it.** Every one of the 16 runs the packed driver, with `plan` and `packed` agreeing to 1.00, because the copy-free faer route is *unavailable*: `strategy/faer.rs::plan` returns `None` unless each GEMM axis is a contiguous group of its operands, and in `abjc-cbka-kj` the contracted `(a,b,c)` sits at axes 0, 1 and 3 of `A`. The planner records `Reason::NotFusable`. | Read of `crates/tcontract/src/strategy/faer.rs` (`plan`, `separate_c_pays`) against the case's label set, plus the recorded `plan == packed` on all 16 rows. A faer bound is irrelevant where faer cannot run. |
| Is it the block-scatter path the corpus exists for? | **No.** The rows report `regular_a`/`regular_b` = 1.00 (0.67/1.00 for two c64 rows), i.e. packing from plain strided panels, and the measured rows already ran the non-gathering writeback - `TCBENCH_WRITEBACK=fast` is the default and `gather` is what has to be asked for. | The cell's own `regular_a`/`regular_b` columns, recorded per row. |
| Is it the blocking model, or its constants? | **No.** `TCBENCH_BLOCKMODEL=analytical` against the shipping legacy constants gives packed/tblis 1.554 against 1.541, 1.612 against 1.611, 1.519 against 1.494 and 1.684 against 1.675 on four probes; `TCBENCH_{MC,NC,KC}` at half and at one and a half times the derived values move `packed` by at most 2.5% and leave the ratio between 1.51 and 1.64. | Same-process A/B with TBLIS in the same run, `pinned.sh`, 1500 ms priming, best of 5, on the measured binary. This is also the answer to whether the *pending* `BlockModel` decision would help here: it would not. |
| Is it per-call overhead? | **No.** The calls are 25-45 ms long, so no constant per-call cost can produce a 1.5x multiplicative gap, and the same call path wins its rows elsewhere - 42/42 on the per-shape corpus, median ratio 0.042. | The row times themselves, and the two corpora side by side on the same pages. |
| So what is left? | **The packed micro-kernels**, against BLIS as driven through TBLIS's TTGT formulation, on exactly the shapes that cannot be fused. No code change is made here: none of the three candidates this campaign listed - the f64 `FaerLimit`, the c64 planar family and blocking, the call path - is the cause, and a kernel is not a configuration change. | Next check, if this class is to be pursued: a single-shape FLOP/s A/B against BLIS `dgemm`, which separates micro-kernel efficiency from the packing, writeback and partition around it. That is a new investigation with its own budget, not a follow-up to this one. |

## 2026-10-09 the harness primed too little, and threw away the scatter ([#91](https://github.com/tensor4all/tcontract-rs/pull/91))

`tcbench` and `lukbench` compare engines measured in one process, per case, in a
fixed order that puts `plan` first, and primed each arm for 500 ms of wall clock.
For a call of about 2 ms that is inside the range `PERFORMANCE_TIPS.md` allows and
too short to settle.

| Question | Ruling | Evidence or next check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Was the 500 ms priming biased, and in which direction? | **Yes, and it penalised whichever arm was measured first — always `plan`.** For `ij-ik-kj` (f64, 1 MiB) the arm measured first read 2.89 ms against 2.06 ms once settled: 40% higher latency, 29% lower rate. An arm measured after another read 2.20 ms where the same work measured alone read 3.08 ms, so it looked 29% faster than it was. The measured symptom is a pair that resolves to the same packed configuration differing by 17-27% (`abcijk-ijma-mkbc` c64 1 MiB 4T: 0.827, 0.73). | `benchmarks/scripts/pinned.sh 4` on the pin checkout, best of 5, three rounds per configuration, priming 500, 1500 and 3000 ms, each arm also measured alone in its own process. Fixed by the 1500 ms default (PR #91); at 1500 ms the pair agrees — six runs of that c64 case give 0.973, 0.978, 0.996, 0.999, 1.031, 1.040 (mean 1.003), and `ij-ik-kj` f64 reads 1.048 at 1T and 1.140 at 4T in `plan`'s favour where the biased pair read 0.827. |
| Does that invalidate the rows already recorded? | **The counts and directions in the sections below were measured with the biased instrument, so their margins are bounds, not values.** A row whose arms were compared inside one 500 ms-primed process can be wrong by tens of percent, and rows quoted at 1.01-1.06x cannot be separated from this host's noise at all. The published campaign cells were re-measured at the fixed priming; the older recordings stay as history and are marked `declaration-changed`. | The 2026-10-09 rows below carry the caveat. `docs/worklog.md` in `tcontract-benchmark` records the re-measurement, and its cells carry the `spread` of every row. |
| What did the harness still hide after the priming fix? | **The scatter across repetitions, and the gap between the frozen policy and the executed geometry.** `timed()` kept only the best of `--reps`, so a first repetition 25-95% slower than the best left no trace; the CSV now carries `spread = (max - min) / best`. The row's `grid=` label reported `PackedReport::partition`, which is the policy the plan froze, not what `driver::route` resolves at call time (an automatic policy's `0x0` sentinel, a pinned grid clamped to the executor's budget, direct-B reshaping, batch lanes): the rows now say `policy=auto\|pin:MxN\|dynamic:JMxJN align=...`. The same-family agreement is a diagnostic to apply by hand — the harness compares outputs, never two arms' timings. | Measured six runs of `abcijk-ijma-mkbc` c64 1 MiB 4T with the new column: spreads 95.1, 24.1, 13.0, 4.7, 32.3, 1.1, 96.6, 61.6, 2.9, 61.1%. Two identical-configuration arms still differ by up to 3.5% through `best` alone, and more across sessions; this is the floor under any claim a cell makes. |

## 2026-10-09 faer against the packed driver, per dtype, width and C mode ([#69](https://github.com/tensor4all/tcontract-rs/issues/69), [#63](https://github.com/tensor4all/tcontract-rs/issues/63))

Three independent recordings of the same configuration — one overwrite-only, one
with the accumulating form added, one with a fixed `FaerLimit::NONE` arm — 79
comparable groups each at 1T and 4T, 500 ms of priming per arm, best of 5, three
idle-gated sessions.

| Question | Ruling | Evidence or next check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Delete faer and route every fusable problem to the packed driver? | **No.** The gate the S0 measurement of #50 set — `faer-subset <= max(1.05, 1+noise)` — is not met. At 1T, across three recordings, **0 of 79 comparable groups have the packed driver ahead in all three**, and the narrowest gap is a tie (c64 `ikb,knb->inb` at n=16, batch=16: 1.01, 0.99 and 1.00 in the three recordings). Packed/planner medians over sessions, newest recording, overwrite form: f32 1.24-5.81, f64 1.15-5.92, c32 1.05-3.56, c64 1.00-1.86. | [faer-limit-sweep](../experiments/faer-limit-sweep/results/manifest.txt): zen5 APU, 1T and 4T, 23 shapes x 4 dtypes x 3 arms x 2 C modes x 3 idle-gated sessions, prebuilt plans and preallocated outputs. `results/audit.py` recomputes every number in this row from the recordings and fails if a claim stops holding. |

| Strategy choice? | **Decided:** by rule, in order: an explicit kernel, selector, partition, complex method, blocking, cache model or write-back request forces the packed driver; else an all-batch problem is elementwise; else a copy-free full-semantics fusion runs on faer; else packed. The old `Strategy`/`Flags`/`EngineChoice` are gone. |
| faer applicability? | **Decided (amended by Phase 2 W2, 2026-10-03):** faer only for fusions that copy no operand and implement the full update: `op_D` is distributed over the sum by conjugation flipping; the previous-D term and a separately described C are written into D by one parallel output-sized strided pass (`D = op_D(beta*op_C(C))`, strided-basic) before faer accumulates the product. That pass is not operand materialization (A, B, C are never copied or packed; `no_materialize` and `PlanReport::materialized` are unchanged). W2b: a separate C goes to faer when the output has at most 2^16 elements, or K >= 512 with A unit-stride along M, or M or N is 1 (`separate_c_pays`, fitted on the W2 rows; the first W2 rule admitted six cases at 0.69-0.92). Otherwise the packed driver serves it at every beta: the pass costs 20-50% of faer's time, measured, and W2b's `beta == 0` faer route was removed after the clean confirmation measured it losing those cases at 4T/8T where there is no pass at all. A reduction one input lacks stays on packed. The old copying branches are deleted. | [W2 results](../benchmarks/benchmarks/tcontract/contract/results/2026-10-03-phase2-w2/decision.txt), [W2b](../benchmarks/benchmarks/tcontract/contract/results/2026-10-03-phase2-w2b/decision.txt), [clean W2b](../benchmarks/benchmarks/tcontract/contract/results/2026-10-04-phase2-w2b-clean/decision.txt) |
| Elementwise semantics? | **Decided:** one strided pass (since #37 follow-up: strided-basic kernels with `op_D` folded into the other conjugations and one dispatch per execution, not a hand-rolled odometer loop with per-element flags; in-place accumulation uses strided-rs's `execution` traversal with a local raw-pointer inner loop, because a checked call would need a second view aliasing the `&mut` destination) with the full `op_D(alpha*op_A*op_B + beta*op_C(C))`, `C` in place, separate or absent; it also serves `alpha == 0` and an empty contraction for every strategy, so `op_D(beta*op_C(C))` is computed identically everywhere and reads no input. |
| Explicit kernel or partition on an all-batch problem? | **Decided (behaviour change):** runs the packed driver. The baseline refused it with `EngineUnsupported`. Tested for `StaticGrid` and `DynamicTiles`. |
| `Partition`? | **Decided:** `StaticGrid { pin, align_c_lines } \| DynamicTiles { job_m, job_n }`; `None` is the default rule. `PartitionMode` is gone (`Rows`, `Cols` and the ungated `Rule` had no retained consumer); `DynamicTiles` + `align_c_lines` is unrepresentable. |
| Selector context? | **Decided:** no thread budget and no candidate grids: a plan is built before an executor is chosen and never reselects for another budget. Operand labels are gone (the problem is label-free after lowering). |
| Width? | **Decided:** chosen at each call from `2 * macs * (4 if complex) * NS_PER_FLOP` and the executor's budget; one `NS_PER_FLOP` (0.05, provisional) replaces the three copies. |
| `contract_batched` schedule? | **Decided:** items at least as many as the width are partitioned over it, each serial; fewer items run one after another, each on the whole `Exec`. All items are validated before any runs. A forced `Split` that asks for item threads inside the lanes is refused with `StrategyUnavailable` before any write, as the packed driver does; this schedule never nests (see `BatchDecision`'s route contract). |
| Raw (C) execution? | **Decided:** `Plan::check_raw` is the pointer-only semantic preflight (output overlap, `C`/`D` aliasing, judged on address ranges); null pointers stay the adapter's. TAPP lowers once and keeps its sequential item loop. |
| Legacy `KernelSet`? | **Decided:** deleted. The planner resolves families from the registry; the default menu is the registry's built-in families of the preferred ISA and scheme, and a test pins that it equals the old dispatch menu entry for entry. |
| Benchmarks? | **Decided:** `tcbench` keeps `run`, `verify`, `info`, `--stress`; `sweep`/`shapes`/`orient`/`premise` are deleted. The three complex methods are a knob, not three engines. GEMM corpora are contraction cases. No timing was run for this PR. |
| All-batch problems after Phase 2? | **Decided by the maintainer, 2026-10-02**, after the per-element-branch defect in the hand-rolled elementwise loop (runtime flags per element, no contiguous inner loop): contractions with a K role use only the packed driver; all-batch (pure elementwise) problems are delegated to strided-rs permanently, with `op_D` folded into the other conjugations and monomorphized flag-free bodies. They are not removed in Phase 2. Shared rules: tensor4all-agent-rules#16 (no runtime mode flags in tensor-sized loops; elementwise work via strided-rs; structural review of inner loops). | Spec D5, section 4.3 rule 2, section 10 |
