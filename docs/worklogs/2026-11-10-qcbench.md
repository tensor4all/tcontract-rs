# qcbench: the QC binary-contraction benchmark member

> Second campaign suite after `tcbench` and `lukbench`. The corpus, the extent
> derivation and the campaign wiring are specified in the benchmark job's
> `docs/tcontract-chem-real-benchmark-plan.md`; this record covers the
> engineering decisions in the harness member itself.

## What was decided before this change

The member is `benchmarks/benchmarks/qcbench/` and reuses `tcbench`'s shared
modules through `#[path]` (`engines/`, `knobs.rs`, `report.rs`, `blas.rs`,
`tblis.rs`, `ttgt.rs`); only the corpus and its tests are qcbench-owned. That
reuse is the point: a qcbench number should be the same measurement discipline
as a tcbench number, with different shapes.

The one shared-module change this member does make is a memory fix, kept
deliberately small and behaviour-preserving: `verify` releases the
forced-packed output once its cross-check is done, and the TBLIS arm of `run`
writes into the plan arm's output (already cloned into the cross-arm
reference) instead of allocating its own. Both buffers were outside the timed
regions, so reported times and comparisons are unchanged, and
`tcbench`/`lukbench verify` still pass. The change cuts a whole-binary case's
peak from A+B+3C to A+B+2C — 14.98 GiB instead of 22.4 GiB for the binding
family — which is what lets an 8 GiB cell run on a 24 GiB host. The `ttgt`
arm is left alone: its buffer use sits inside the timed closure.

## Choices made here

- **One literal two-operand einsum per case**, transcribed from the corpus
  `cases.csv` files, with the corpus's own index letters and a per-case
  domain table. The 24 cases are the two most expensive formal-scaling rungs
  of nine corpora (RCCSD, RCCSD(T), EOM-IP, EOM-EA, CCSD gradient, RI-HF
  energy and gradients, TDHF/CIS response, MO RI-J/K response).
- **Packed domains stay packed.** The papers print triangular pair arrays
  (`vroot = v(v-1)/2`, `ao_root = ao(ao-1)/2`, …), and the corpus rows declare
  those extents. Unpacking them would change the MAC count and footprint the
  suite exists to measure, so `Dom` carries the domain and the harness sizes
  the packed index directly. The occupied-pair index is the exception:
  `gradient.X`/`gradient.Z_pair` are dense `(o, o, naux)` because the corpus
  row is dense there and a packed `ij` would measure a caller-side layout
  choice rather than a contraction.
- **The footprint budget is asserted, not assumed.** `corpus/tests.rs` pins
  the 8 GiB whole-binary cell budget, the `naux = round(k*(o+v))` rule, the
  three ratio extents, and that the binding case is the `o^3 v^3` triples
  output of `T.connected.virtual`.
- **f64 only.** The harness rejects `c64` rather than skipping it; the
  complex-orbital benchmark is a separate plan over separate corpora.

## Verification

`cargo test --profile ci -p tcontract-bench --bin qcbench` covers the corpus
selection, the einsum letters, the domain table, the derivation rules and the
footprint budget. The campaign records `qcbench verify` (all families, 1T and
4T) before any timing evidence; see the campaign repository.

## Corpus note

`ri_rhf_gradient_corpus`'s `gradient.X` row was de-packed to dense `(o, o,
naux)` on 2026-11-10 (it previously declared a packed `o_sym` shape against
dense `naux, naux` operand shapes). That ties it with `gradient.Z_pair` on the
`o^2*naux^2` rung, so the corpus now contributes three cases.
