## Decision
- Rule (one function, strategy/faer.rs `separate_c_pays`): a separate C goes to faer for every beta when out <= 2^16, or K >= 512 with A unit-stride along M, or M == 1 or N == 1. Otherwise the plan is packed for beta != 0 and keeps a faer route for beta == 0 (PlanReport::beta_zero = Some(Faer)); one branch on `beta == 0` per call.
- Fitted on the W2 rows: the old rule admitted six cases below 0.92 (017, 032, 041, 042, 043, 044); the new one admits none, at the cost of declining wins such as gemm_batched_047 (out 158400, M = 1 is kept by the matvec arm) and the 2^16..2^20 outputs with K < 512 that happened to win.
- Confirmation (s1 above, loaded host): no faer-routed case below 0.93 at any width (0.93/0.94/0.95 = within the 6-26% A/A of the same session); faer-routed workload packed/plan 1.01-1.16.
- Caveats: one session, host shared with unpinned jobs (A/A of identical code up to x1.4-3 on single cases), b0 measured on 1 case only (equivalence with W2 b0), thresholds fit on f64 plus the large-batched c64 entries.
