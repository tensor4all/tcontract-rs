Predeclared (before the campaign): keep if complex or f64 packed-route cases gain without any case regressing beyond
the A/A noise at 1T/4T/8T; neutral = no gain, then not merged.

Change: pack's conj flag is a const generic (resolved once per panel call), write-back's beta=0 / alpha=1 / conj_c /
conj_d flags are a const MODE (resolved once per tile; PLAIN, SCALE, SCALE_CD, four ACC, and SUM for real storage with
alpha=beta=1); pack and write-back have `#[target_feature(enable = "avx2,fma")]` variants (x86_64) bound with the
family (ResolvedGemm::simd, decided once at bind time). Tests compare every (format, mode, row stride, simd, gather)
variant bitwise to an independent scalar loop. Results are bitwise unchanged.

Measured (table.txt, s1+s2, packed_exec, ratio after/before):
- 1T geomean 1.004 over 20 cases; the untouched plan (faer) row moves 1.008 (host drift). c64: gemm_1024_c64 0.959,
  dot_general_031_c64 0.949, gemm_batched_009_c64 0.951, dot_general_016_c64 0.992 (about -5% on three of four; consistent across both sessions).
- f64 (1024^3, dot_general_000/001/005/007, gemm_batched_008/012/022/023): 0.99-1.03, no gain.
- 4T geomean 1.013 (ctl 1.022), 8T geomean 1.019 (ctl 1.015): no regression beyond the control. gemm_1024_f64 at 8T
  read 1.106 in both sessions, with its own control at 1.054-1.065; a targeted pinned repeat (3 alternations x 3
  binaries, 7 runs each) gave before 6.93-7.22 ms, final code 7.13-7.44 ms, first W3a build 6.29-7.11 ms: not reproduced
  as a regression (noise about +-5%).
- Why f64 does not gain: perf on 1024^3 f64 puts write-back at 3.3% and pack at 1.7% of all samples (both rows;
  about 7% and 3.5% of the packed row), and pack in the pack-heavy cases (008, n4_k32) is dominated by strided gather
  latency, not by what the compiler can vectorize. The accumulate loop of write-back stays scalar (vmulsd/vaddsd) because C and D alias.
